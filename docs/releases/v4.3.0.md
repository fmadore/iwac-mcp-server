# v4.3.0: explore_corpus pages its cell views

`explore_corpus` takes `limit` (default 20, max 50) and `offset`, but its
`compare`, `coverage` and `attention` modes ignored both and returned fixed caps
of 40 categories, 400 cells and 200 cells. Asking `attention` for five rows on
the *Terrorisme* selection returned 200 cells, about 8,000 tokens. Found while
testing v4.2.0 end to end through the installed server.

- **The model reads one page.** The three cell views now return `limit` rows
  from `offset`, with `has_more` and a new `total_cells`, so a client pages
  through the ranking the same way it pages records. On the real dataset,
  `attention` at `limit: 5` drops from about 8,000 tokens to about 210, and
  `coverage` to about 300.
- **The chart keeps its picture.** The chart still receives the largest 40
  categories, 400 coverage cells or 200 attention cells through the widget-only
  `_meta` channel, along with `omitted_cells`, whatever page the model asked
  for. `omitted_cells` is therefore no longer in the model's payload.
- **Compare pages by category.** Its cells are ordered by combined category
  count, A before B within a category, so a page never splits a category from
  its counterpart. The year-by-year `temporal` series is unchanged: it is the
  answer to "how do these compare over time", not overflow.
- **Tests.** The fixture suite checks, for all three modes, that a page is the
  requested size, is a prefix of the full ranking, continues correctly from
  `offset`, and leaves the chart's cell set intact.

Other tools and the dataset are unchanged.
